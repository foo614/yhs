# Public website company logo

FOO-183 uses the company artwork supplied on 2026-09-27. `ys-heng-company-logo-original.png` is the unchanged source copy.

The public PNG is cropped to `(360, 408, 875, 784)` from the 1599 by 1600 source. Neutral background pixels (all RGB channels at least 235 and a channel spread of at most 3) are transparent; the remaining artwork pixels retain their original RGB values. The design, lettering, company name and proportions are preserved. No artwork was generated or redrawn.

Browser icons and social previews are proportionately resized from that asset on white. The public header, footer, secondary navigation and showroom enquiry page use the same full artwork. The fine company-name lettering is clearest in the larger footer and social versions; small browser icons and compact navigation necessarily render it much smaller. The dark lettering is intended for the existing light website surfaces, not an inverted dark variant.

The home hero signboards and the showroom's desktop illustration and mobile composition also contained the old artwork. Those logo regions are directly composited with the supplied mark, with perspective on the signboards and interpolation of the adjacent plain background in the showroom. The vehicles, layout and interactive targets are retained; no AI generation is used. Existing small text-only dealer number plates remain unchanged.
